Engineering for the Community

My mission is to earn my degree in engineering so that I may build a more inclusive world, where technical solutions are always grounded in human empathy.

  • Advocating for residentsTurning residents' struggles into government appeals at weekly Meet-the-People Sessions.
  • Grassroots coordinationAddressing daily community hurdles with the Siglap Youth Network and the Eastwood Ria Network.
  • Connecting with peopleLearning to communicate with heart as an emcee.
Read MoreShow Less

Why engineering? I believe that the most profound technical skills are those that include the human element. The world faces deep social and ethical problems that require us to be directly on the ground. This is why I immerse myself in my community today: I want to understand exactly how the real world works before I begin to build for it.

Evidence of commitment. My experiences have taught me that true problem solving begins with listening. I am dedicated to bridging the gap between pure logic and the people it is meant to serve.

At weekly Meet the People Sessions, I translate emotional struggles into actionable government appeals. This role has taught me real problem solving beyond textbooks. Working with the Siglap Youth Network and Eastwood Ria Network, I identify and address daily community hurdles. As an emcee, I focus on understanding human interaction: managing room energy or small groups, learning to communicate with heart.

“The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others.”Mahatma Gandhi
